# Practical activity — IAM and RBAC

## Steps
1. Open the resource group containing your lab resources.
2. Select **Access control (IAM)**.
3. Inspect **View my access** and existing role assignments.
4. Identify the principal, role, and scope.
5. Read the managed identity guide.
6. When the app is deployed, enable its managed identity and assign only the specific permissions it needs.

Do not change your own access or grant broad roles as part of exploration.

## Completion checklist
- [ ] I understand authentication and authorization.
- [ ] I can identify principal, role, and scope.
- [ ] I understand least privilege.
- [ ] I understand why managed identity is safer than embedding credentials.
